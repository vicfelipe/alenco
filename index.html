01 · THE MISSION

EARTH IS HUNGRY.

The ASTRON corporation found the answer on Titan Moon: the Moon Radish, a crop that grows nowhere else.

They sent one farmer. Billy. One rocket, the Astron One, and a promise to bring him home.

But Titan is not empty. The Moonsters were here first, and they want the harvest. Void portals tear open in the ground. And somewhere past round ten, something bigger is waiting.

Harvest. Return. Repeat. Twenty rounds. One way home.

02 · HOW TO PLAY

FIVE RADISHES.
ONE ROCKET.

01

HARVEST

Walk over the five Moon Radishes of each round. They follow you wherever you go.

02

RETURN

Bring the harvest to the Astron One to clear the round. If a Moonster touches a radish, it drops.

03

FREEZE

Grab Moon Ore to load the Time Freeze Gun. Two shots. Frozen Moonsters can't hurt you.

04

REWIND

Made a mistake? The Retrosuit turns back time. Charges are limited every round.
